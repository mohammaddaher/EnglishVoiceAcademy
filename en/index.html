Exam & Academic Preparation

Structured preparation for international English tests and school curricula. Lessons focus on the skills each exam assesses, its question types, and strategies for managing your time.

  • IELTS

    Preparation for all four skills — Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking — with practice in task types, timing and test strategy.

  • TOEFL

    Focused practice for TOEFL iBT reading, listening, speaking and writing tasks, with attention to academic vocabulary and note-taking.

  • SAT English

    Support for the SAT Reading and Writing section: understanding passages, grammar and usage, and answering questions precisely.

  • Duolingo English Test

    Get familiar with the test’s adaptive question types and build the quick, flexible English skills it measures.

  • PTE Academic

    Practice for integrated speaking, writing, reading and listening tasks, including the computer-based question formats.

  • AP English

    Support for AP English Language and AP English Literature: close reading, analysis and timed essay writing.

  • Edexcel English

    Help for students following Pearson Edexcel English courses, with a focus on reading, writing and exam technique.

  • IB English

    Guidance for IB English courses, developing textual analysis, essay writing and oral skills.

English Voice Academy offers preparation courses only. We are not an official test centre — tests are registered for and taken through the official test providers.

About the academy

An English academy in Amman, Jordan

English Voice Academy helps learners in Amman use English with confidence — in conversation, in the classroom and in exams. Our Arabic name, أكاديمية اللسان المطلق, reflects the same goal: fluent, confident expression.

We welcome adults, school and university students, and children, and we help each learner choose a program that fits their goals and current level.

  • Speaking confidence

    Every course makes room for using English, not just studying it.

  • Clear exam focus

    Preparation built around the skills and formats of each test.

  • Learning for all ages

    From playful children’s lessons to courses for adults.
